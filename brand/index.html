01 The idea

One shape, two meanings.

The mark is the business model drawn as simply as possible. It survives a 16px favicon and a billboard.

  1. The appAn iOS-style squircle in ink. It stands for the developer's product, always in front.
  2. The coinA coin rising behind the top-right corner. It is the backing: capital that supports the app without taking its place.
  3. The badgeA circle on an app's top-right corner is also the notification badge everyone taps. Attention, by design.
  4. The gapA clean cut separates app and coin. We stand behind you; we don't merge with you. Ownership stays yours.

Do

  • Clear space around the logo equals the coin's radius.
  • Minimum size: mark 16px, lockup 96px wide.
  • Put the coin in the field's contrast colour.
  • Use the outlined files. Never retype the wordmark.

Don't

  • Recolour the coin lime or gradient it.
  • Rotate, outline, add shadows or glow to the flat mark.
  • Move the coin to another corner or close the gap.
  • Put the ink app on a dark field. Use the on-ink version.

08 Voice

Short. Specific. Unafraid.

Numbers before adjectives. Say the catch before the reader finds it. Never promise rankings.

We spend. You grow.The offer in four words. Always followed by who keeps what.
We split the difference.The idiom and the deal in one line: half of what we add, nothing of what you had.
That's our risk, not yours.Say what happens when it fails. It is the most persuasive line on the page.
Money is fuel. The team is the engine.The growth team is the moat. Say it plainly, without hype words.
