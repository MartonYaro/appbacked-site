02 Principles

How we behave.

Written down so you can hold us to them.

01

We get paid when you grow.

Our share comes only from revenue above your line. No fees, no repayments. If we add nothing, we earn nothing.

02

Half, then nothing.

50/50 on growth for 24 months, then we step away. In every deal, in writing. We never invent a reason to take more.

03

Your app stays yours.

No equity, no IP, no say in your roadmap. The users we pay for and the ad accounts we build are yours too.

04

Say the catch first.

If there's a condition, you hear it before you sign. Our contracts are short enough to read in one sitting.
