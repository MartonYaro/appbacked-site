App Store locales and locale codes.

The 50 localizations App Store Connect supports, the locale code for each one, and which languages every one of the 175 App Store storefronts shows. Taken from Apple's own tables, in one place.

AppBacked growth teamUpdated October 26, 2026Reference

On this page

  1. All 50 locale codes
  2. Five facts from the table
  3. Languages by storefront
  4. Where the codes are used
  5. FAQ

A localization is a language version of your product page: its own name, subtitle, keywords, description and screenshots. A storefront is a country's App Store. Each storefront has one default language and often a few more. Which localization a person sees depends on the storefront, their device language and the localizations you've added. If you're deciding which languages to add, start with our guide to App Store localization strategy; this page is the reference behind it.

Five facts from the table

  1. English (U.K.) is the default in 135 of 175 storefronts and is shown in 172. If your U.K. listing is a copy of the U.S. one, or missing, that is the page most of the world sees.
  2. French reaches 32 storefronts, more than any language except English (U.K.), largely across Africa, the Middle East and Europe.
  3. Spanish (Mexico) is the default across Latin America: 16 storefronts, and 19 show it. Spanish (Spain) is shown only in Spain.
  4. Arabic is shown in 17 storefronts, across the Gulf and North Africa, but is the default in none of them.
  5. 71 storefronts show only one language. The United States shows ten: English (U.S.) plus Arabic, both Chinese scripts, French, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico) and Vietnamese.

Where the codes are used

  • App Store Connect API: the locale attribute of app info and version localizations takes these codes.
  • Metadata tools: folder-per-language tools such as fastlane's deliver name each folder with the same code, for example metadata/en-US/ or metadata/zh-Hans/.
  • Your own spreadsheets: keep the code next to each translated field. It removes the “French or French (Canada)?” question when someone uploads.

The codes name store localizations, not your app's languages. The languages the app itself supports come from the localizations in your Xcode project and show as the “Languages” line on the product page.

FAQ

How many locales does the App Store support?

App Store Connect supports 50 localizations for product pages, each with its own locale code, from ar-SA for Arabic to vi for Vietnamese.

What is the App Store locale code for Simplified Chinese?

zh-Hans. Traditional Chinese is zh-Hant.

What is the difference between en-US and en-GB on the App Store?

They are separate localizations. English (U.S.), en-US, is the default in the United States. English (U.K.), en-GB, is the default in 135 of 175 storefronts, so it is the page most countries outside the U.S. see.

Which languages does the U.S. App Store show?

English (U.S.) by default, plus Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), French, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico) and Vietnamese.

Which language does the App Store show in a country without its own localization?

The storefront's default, which in 135 storefronts is English (U.K.). If you haven't added that localization, your primary language is shown instead.
