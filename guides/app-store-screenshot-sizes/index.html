App Store screenshot sizes for 2026.

Every size Apple accepts right now, the two you actually need to design, the new iPhone Duo sizes, Google Play's rules, and what to put in each of your 10 frames.

Apple accepts more than thirty screenshot sizes, but you only need to design two or three of them. App Store Connect scales the rest down for older and smaller devices. Here is the full list, checked against Apple's specification page, and the short list worth designing.

The short answer

iPhone: design at 1320 × 2868 px (the largest Dynamic Island size) and also export 1206 × 2622 px, because Apple's list of required sizes asks for at least one Dynamic Island (medium display) screenshot.

iPad: if your app runs on iPad, upload 2064 × 2752 px or 2048 × 2732 px. Smaller iPads use scaled versions.

Everything: PNG or JPEG, no transparency, 1 to 10 screenshots per device size.

If your interface looks the same across sizes and languages, Apple says the highest required resolution is enough and it will scale it down. Upload the smaller sizes yourself only when the layout really changes, for example when text would get too small to read.

The 4-inch and 3.5-inch classes also accept versions without the status bar (640 × 1096 and 640 × 920 in portrait). The device column is our shorthand; Apple's page lists display classes, not models.

How the fallbacks work

Apple's notes chain the classes together. If you don't provide Dynamic Island (large) screenshots, the App Store uses scaled Face ID (large) ones, and the other way round. Medium classes fall back to larger ones, and the Home Button classes fall back step by step down to the 3.5-inch display. That is why one large set plus the required medium set covers every iPhone.

iPhone Duo screenshot sizes

Apple has added a class for iPhone Duo with two displays, each with its own size:

DisplayPortrait (px)Landscape (px)
Outer display1398 × 20342034 × 1398
Inner display2007 × 28532853 × 2007

Two things matter. From April 2027, Apple will require iPhone Duo screenshots for any app built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later. And apps that submit them get a badge on their product page saying they are optimized for iPhone Duo. The badge is a reason to do it early: a badge next to a competitor without one is free conversion.

File rules

  • Formats: .png, .jpg or .jpeg.
  • No transparency: images can't include an alpha channel. Export PNGs flattened, without alpha.
  • How many: at least 1 and up to 10 per device size, per localization.
  • Exact pixels: the upload is rejected if the size is off by even one pixel. Export at exactly the sizes above.
  • Review rules apply: screenshots must follow the App Review Guidelines, so show the app in use, not only marketing art.

App preview video specs

App previews are short videos that play before the screenshots. They are optional, but on a crowded results page motion catches the eye.

  • Count: up to 3 per device size and language.
  • Length: 15 to 30 seconds. The default poster frame is at 5 seconds.
  • File: up to 500 MB, H.264 (.mov, .m4v, .mp4) or ProRes 422 HQ (.mov), up to 30 fps.
  • Resolution: 886 × 1920 portrait for all current iPhone classes, 1200 × 1600 for iPads from 10.5 to 13 inches, 1920 × 1080 for Mac and Apple TV, 3840 × 2160 for Apple Vision Pro.

Note that previews use their own accepted resolutions, which are not the screen resolutions. A 1320 × 2868 screen recording has to be scaled to 886 × 1920 before upload.

A 1320 × 2868 iPhone frame is about 9:19.5, which Google Play accepts but which isn't the 9:16 it asks for promotion eligibility. Re-lay your frames on a 1080 × 1920 canvas instead of reusing the iPhone files.

The 10-frame playbook

Sizes get you through review. The order of the frames decides the install. In search results people see only the first frames, and many never open the gallery, so we plan the slots like this:

  1. The outcome. What life looks like with the app, in five words or fewer. Not the logo, not “Welcome”.
  2. The main feature people pay for, shown in use.
  3. Proof: a rating, an award, a number of users, a press quote. Only real ones.
  4. Frames 4 to 7: one feature per frame, each answering a question people ask in reviews.
  5. Frame 8: what's free and what's paid, so trial users aren't surprised.
  6. Frames 9 and 10: integrations, widgets, platforms, privacy.

Then localize the captions for every language you sell in, and test the first three frames with Product Page Optimization before you redesign the rest. That is why the first frame is the first thing we test.

FAQ

What is the App Store screenshot size for iPhone in 2026?

Design at 1320 × 2868 px, the largest Dynamic Island size, and also export 1206 × 2622 px, because Apple's required list asks for at least one Dynamic Island (medium display) screenshot. The App Store scales these for every other iPhone.

Do I still need 6.5-inch screenshots?

Only if you don't upload Dynamic Island (large) screenshots. Apple marks the Face ID (large) class, which covers 1284 × 2778 and 1242 × 2688, as required when the Dynamic Island (large) set is missing.

What is the iPad screenshot size for the App Store?

2064 × 2752 px or 2048 × 2732 px for the 13-inch class, which Apple requires if your app runs on iPad. Smaller iPads use scaled versions.

How many screenshots can I upload to the App Store?

Up to 10 per device size and localization, and at least one. Google Play allows up to 8 per device type.

Can App Store screenshots have transparent backgrounds?

No. Apple rejects images with an alpha channel. Export flattened PNG or JPEG files.
