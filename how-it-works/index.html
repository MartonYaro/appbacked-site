AppBacked / How it works

Our money.
Your growth.
Half the upside.

We invest our own budget and team in your app. You keep everything you make today. We share only revenue above your current line, for 24 months, and then we step away.

02 Step by step

Test first. Then scale.

We spend in small steps and only keep spending where it pays back. Nobody is locked into a bet that isn't working.

  1. 10 seconds

    X-ray

    Paste your link. See the obvious levers on your store page.

  2. 48 hours

    Growth plan

    We read your reports and send a budget, the channels and what we fix first.

  3. Month 1

    Test, on us

    Store page, paywall and small paid tests. Either side can walk away after it. Nobody owes anything.

  4. Months 2–24

    Scale and split

    We grow what pays back. Revenue above the line is split 50/50. You get a statement every month.

  5. Month 25

    All yours

    We step away. The growth, the ad accounts and every user we paid for stay with you.

03 Follow the money

Our money never touches your account.

We pay for growth directly. Revenue comes in as usual. Only what's above your line gets shared.

Where our money goesUp to 6×

Into growth. Not into your bank.

We run and fund the campaigns from our side and stop anything that doesn't pay back. Creatives and ad accounts are handed over when we step away.

Where the revenue goes50/50

Your line stays yours.

The line is your average net store revenue over the three months before we start. A month below the line means our share is zero.

Your app never leaves your developer account. We work from read-only access to your store reports and analytics, and release the budget month by month as the growth shows up. Your code, brand, users and IP stay yours.

05 Fair both ways

What we look at. What we ask.

We bet our own money, so we look hard before we do. Then we ask for very little.

What we look at

  • Six months or more of store revenue history.
  • Retention, refunds and how steady the curve is.
  • The levers: store page, paywall, pricing, untapped markets.
  • Monetization that already works: subscriptions, purchases or ads.
  • Your standing with Apple and Google.

What we ask in return

  • Keep the app live and updated during the term.
  • Keep its revenue in the store, not in a side channel.
  • Read-only access to your store reports and analytics.
  • Pay our share each month, after your store payout lands.
