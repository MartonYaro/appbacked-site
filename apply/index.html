01 No surprises

What we'll ask for. And what we won't.

We look at your app's numbers, not your credit history. Here is the whole list.

To make an offer

  • Your App Store or Google Play link.
  • A monthly revenue range. Exact numbers come later.
  • After a first call: read-only revenue reports, through an App Store Connect API key with the Finance role or Play Console financial reports.
  • Access to your analytics or attribution tool, if you use one.

What we never ask for

  • Your Apple ID or Google password. Access always goes through roles you control.
  • Your source code or your users' personal data.
  • Equity, a board seat or a say in your product.
  • Money. Not to apply, not to start, not ever upfront.
