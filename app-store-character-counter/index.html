Why keywords are bytes

App Store Connect measures the keyword field in bytes of UTF-8 text. Latin letters take one byte, so in English 100 bytes is about 100 characters. Accented letters such as é or ü take two, Cyrillic and Greek take two, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters take three, so you fit roughly 33 of them. Three habits waste the field:

  • Spaces after commas. “photo, editor” spends a byte the search doesn't need.
  • Repeats. Each word only needs to appear once.
  • Words already in the name or subtitle. Apple combines these fields, so repeating them in keywords adds nothing.

FAQ

How many characters is the App Store keyword field?

It's 100 bytes, not characters. In English that's about 100 characters; in Japanese, Chinese or Korean it's about 33.

What is the App Store subtitle character limit?

30 characters, the same as the app name. Both are indexed for search.

Should I put spaces after commas in App Store keywords?

No. Separate keywords with commas only; each space costs a byte you could use for another word.

What is the Google Play short description limit?

80 characters. The full description allows 4,000, and the app name 30.
