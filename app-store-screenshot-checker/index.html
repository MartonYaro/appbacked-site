Check your screenshots

Files are read in your browser and never uploaded. Sizes come from Apple's screenshot specifications and Google Play Console Help, the same sources as our guide to App Store screenshot sizes.

What it checks

  • Exact pixels for the App Store. App Store Connect rejects a screenshot that is off by a single pixel. The checker matches each file against every accepted iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Vision Pro and Apple Watch size, portrait and landscape, and shows the nearest size when there is no match.
  • The sets you actually need. A large iPhone set (6.9-inch or 6.5-inch), the 6.3-inch Dynamic Island set, and 13-inch iPad screenshots if your app runs on iPad.
  • Google Play rules. Each side between 320 and 3,840 px, the long side no more than twice the short side, and whether the frame is promotion-ready: 16:9 or 9:16 at 1,080 px or more.
  • File problems. Formats other than PNG and JPEG, and PNGs with an alpha channel or transparency, which Apple rejects.

FAQ

Why does App Store Connect reject my screenshot?

Usually because the size isn't one Apple accepts, even by one pixel, or because a PNG has an alpha channel. Export at an exact size from the list, as a flattened PNG or a JPEG.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. The checker reads the files in your browser to get their size and format. Nothing is sent to our server.

What screenshot size should I design for the App Store in 2026?

1320 × 2868 px for iPhone, plus 1206 × 2622 px for the 6.3-inch class, and 2064 × 2752 px for iPad if your app runs on iPad.

Can I reuse iPhone screenshots on Google Play?

Usually not. Google Play rejects images whose long side is more than twice the short side, and a 1320 × 2868 iPhone frame is about 2.2 times as tall as it is wide. Re-lay the frames on a 1080 × 1920 canvas, which is also the 9:16 Google asks for promotion eligibility.
